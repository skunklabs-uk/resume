## CV tone

The CV should sound like a senior engineering leader who understands how banks actually work.

It should not sound like:

- a prompt-engineering enthusiast;
- a junior AI developer;
- a pure DevOps engineer;
- an ML scientist;
- a generic project manager.

Desired tone:

**senior, pragmatic, technical, regulated, international, delivery-oriented.**
